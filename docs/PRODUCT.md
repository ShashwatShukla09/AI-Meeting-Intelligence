# Product Decisions

AI Meeting Intelligence was built around one problem:

**Meetings create decisions, but execution gets lost after the call ends.**

The product turns meeting conversations into structured, trackable information while keeping a human in control.

## Human-in-the-Loop

AI can misinterpret an owner, deadline, priority or decision.

Instead of automatically pushing AI-generated content into company tools, every meeting passes through a review layer where the user can edit and approve the output before distribution.

## Structured, Not Just Summarised

The system extracts information teams can act on:

- Decisions
- Action items
- Owners
- Deadlines
- Priorities
- Risks
- Follow-ups

This turns a meeting from a document into something that can be tracked.

## Local AI

Meeting recordings can contain sensitive internal information.

Transcription and AI processing are designed to run locally, reducing the need to send meeting content to external AI services.

## Execution Dashboard

Meeting notes are useful immediately after a call. Actions need visibility afterwards.

The dashboard keeps meetings, ownership, priorities and completion status trackable beyond the original conversation.

## Distribution

Approved meeting intelligence can be distributed to:

- Slack
- Notion
- Email

The goal is to bring the output into tools the team already uses rather than introduce another place they have to check.
